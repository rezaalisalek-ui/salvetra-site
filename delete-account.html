Delete Your Salvetra Account

If you would like to delete your Salvetra account and associated pet data, please contact us by email at support@salvetra.com.

What to include in your request

Please send your request from the email address connected to your Salvetra account. In your message, write that you want your Salvetra account and associated data to be deleted.

Data that will be deleted

After receiving and verifying your request, we will delete your account information and the data associated with your account, including pet profiles, health records, wellness records, medication records, preventive care records, and related app data.

Processing time

Account deletion requests are usually processed within 7 business days after we receive and verify the request.

Data retention

Some limited information may be retained if required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or compliance purposes. Any retained information will only be kept for as long as necessary for those purposes.

Deleting your account is permanent. Once your account and associated data are deleted, they cannot be restored.
