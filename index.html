What We Are Building

Salvetra combines veterinary-guided nutrition, food science, medication administration workflows, subscription wellness systems, and future AI-assisted healthcare optimization into a scalable European Pet HealthTech platform.

Personalized Fresh Nutrition

Customized fresh meal plans based on breed, age, weight, lifestyle, medical condition, allergies, and veterinary assessment.

Medication Adherence

Veterinary-guided support systems designed to improve oral medication administration and treatment compliance.

Digital Care Infrastructure

Planned owner dashboards, adherence tracking, reminder systems, pet health records, and veterinary workflow tools.

AI-Assisted Health Optimization

Future recommendation systems for personalized pet wellness, nutrition optimization, and long-term healthcare support.
