Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 13, 2026

Salvetra respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Salvetra mobile application and website.

Information We Collect

  • Email address used for account registration.
  • Pet information provided by users, including pet profiles, health logs, wellness records, and preventive care reminders.

How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve Salvetra services.
  • To store and display pet health information.
  • To maintain your account and app functionality.

Data Storage

Information is securely stored using Supabase infrastructure.

Data Retention

We retain your account information and pet-related data for as long as your account remains active or as long as necessary to provide Salvetra services.

If you request deletion of your account, we will permanently delete or anonymize your personal information and associated pet data within 30 days, unless we are legally required to retain certain information.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except as required by law.

Children's Privacy

Salvetra is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Contact us with any privacy-related questions.
